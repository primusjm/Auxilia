Britam AKI Awards
The AKI Awards is Kenya's insurance industry's benchmark for recognising excellence. Britam commissioned a dedicated microsite to showcase its award history, celebrate winning agents, and reinforce its position as a trusted, innovation-led industry leader.

Strategy

The objective was to turn a static list of award wins into a page that builds credibility on first glance, for two very different audiences at once: prospective customers evaluating trust, and prospective financial advisors evaluating whether to join the team.

To do this, we structured the page around proof at every scroll depth — headline stats up top (19 years, 2400+ advisors, 156 individual accolades), followed by current-year wins, then named individual recognition, then a browsable archive of past awards. Each layer answers a different question: “Are they credible?”, “Do they reward performance?”, “Is this consistent, not a one-off?”

This approach turned what could have been a dry accolades list into a layered trust-building narrative.

Homepage

Key Features

Stat Bar: Four headline numbers (years of recognition, advisors qualified, individual accolades, company awards) presented immediately, giving visitors an instant credibility signal before any detail.

Company & Individual Award Grids: Current-year wins separated into company-level and individual-level recognition, letting visitors scan by what matters to them — brand trust vs. career opportunity.

Interactive Award Archive: A “tap to reveal” past-awards section that keeps the page scannable while still giving visitors access to the full history on demand.

Media Gallery: Embedded video highlights and a photo gallery from award ceremonies, adding a human, celebratory layer to what would otherwise be a list of titles.

Recruitment Tie-In: A “Join our award-winning team” CTA placed after the proof points, turning the awards page into a recruitment funnel rather than a standalone PR piece.

Outcome

The AKI Awards microsite gives Britam a single, structured home for 19 years of industry recognition — turning scattered PR wins into an ongoing credibility asset that supports both customer trust and advisor recruitment.
