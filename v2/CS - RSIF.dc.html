01The mandate

Talent is everywhere. The calls had to reach it.

Why a pan-African fund needed a clearer, multilingual front door.

3 things to solve
The problem

Scholarships and grants were open to applicants across the continent. Many never found the call, or found it in the wrong language.

The brief

A website that puts scholarships, research and innovation grants one click away, shows who has benefited, and reports results openly.

What it had to prove

That Africa-led science is world-class, and that the fund is open with every partner.

02What we found

Three people,
one fund.

We mapped who visits, and what each needs from the fund.

Research & content
africa’s future science leaders

The PhD applicant

Needs eligibility, deadlines and the apply button, in their own language.

The host university

Wants to see scholars, projects and partners, so they can plan their part.

The funding government

Looks for results against targets and where the money has gone.

03Decisions & tradeoffs

Four calls that shaped the site.

What we chose to lead with, and why.

4 decisions
Decision 01

Three calls, three clear doors

PhD scholarships, research grants, innovation grants. Each has its own banner on the homepage and its own call page.

On the site: the right call in one click.

Decision 02

Speak three languages

English, French and Portuguese, so applicants from Senegal to Mozambique read the call as clearly as those in Kenya.

On the site: one switch, three audiences.

Decision 03

Make the scholars the story

Current students, grant beneficiaries and alumni each get a page, and their research leads the news.

On the site: people first, programme second.

Decision 04

Report results in the open

A results framework and an interactive impact map show progress on key targets for every funder to see.

On the site: transparency built into the menu.
