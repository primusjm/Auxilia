04Method

Checkpoints at every phase, so there are no surprises at the end.

Each phase ends with something you can review, approve or send back before we move on.

Sign-off at every phase
Phase 011–2 weeks

Discovery

We learn who you serve, who funds you, and what has to be true for this to count as a success.

  • Stakeholder interviews: staff, board, beneficiaries
  • Audit of your current site, data and tools
  • Donor and funder reporting needs mapped
You leave withA written problem statement and success measures
Your time: 2–3 hours across two workshops
Phase 021 week

Planning

Scope, platform and timeline agreed in writing, including what we are deliberately not building.

  • Sitemap, user flows and feature list
  • Platform and hosting recommendation
  • Phase dates tied to your funding cycle
You leave withFixed scope, budget and phase dates
Your time: One 90-minute review
Phase 036–12 weeks

Design & build

Design and full-stack development under one roof, reviewed with you at every phase gate.

  • Wireframes, then full visual design
  • Build in two-week sprints with a live preview
  • Tested on low-end phones and slow networks
You leave withWorking site or app, reviewed at each gate
Your time: A 45-minute check-in every two weeks
Phase 041 week

Handover

Documentation and training that leave your team self-reliant, never dependent on us for basics.

  • Launch, redirects and analytics set-up
  • Written guides for every editing task
  • Recorded training for future staff
You leave withDocs, a training session and admin access
Your time: One half-day training session
