02The trip

From list to till

Swipe through the trip
01Starting out
List templatesSave a list you buy often, or start from a pack.Or tap Weekly basics and the list fills itself.
02While listing
Item historyEvery brand, size and price you have paid, one tap away.Each one shows where you bought it, and when.
03While listing
Choose iconAlmost 250 pictures, matched in English or Swahili.Type "sukuma" and the right picture appears.
04While listing
Choose categoryAisles suggested as you type, so the list sorts itself.Shopping in aisle order means no backtracking.
05Before you go
Pick today's shopChoose where you are shopping, and every price follows.Add a new shop in a tap, right from the list.
06Before you go
Cheaper shopSwitch shop and the list re-prices itself.Old prices are struck through so you see why.
07Before you go
Split a tripSplit across two shops when it saves enough.Below your amount, it keeps you to one shop.
08In store
Edit in the aislePrice changed on the shelf? Fix it where you stand.The new price is saved for that shop only.
09At the till
Trip receiptEvery item you bought, grouped by aisle, with the total.Anything not found moves to your next trip.
10At the till
Scan the till slipScan the slip to save what you really paid.The photo is read on your phone, then discarded.
11After
Next timeAnything you skipped waits here for the next trip.Frequently bought items sit on top, one tap to add.
12Anytime
Home Screen widgetsYour total and what is left, without opening the app.The large widget lists every item, ticked as you go.
